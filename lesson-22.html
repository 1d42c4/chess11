About 10–15 minutes with the task · Read, attempt, then explain

The idea

A missed move can come from not seeing an attack, forgetting a piece during calculation, misjudging an exchange, or running out of time. Those problems need different exercises. Record the exact board fact you missed and the habit that might catch it next time. Review a small number of recurring mistakes rather than collecting a huge list of engine inaccuracies.

Worked explanation

“Lost a rook” describes the result. “I moved the defender without checking the rook behind it” identifies a process error and suggests a final-scan exercise.

Try it yourself

Choose one recent mistake. Fill in: trigger, missed fact, correct question, and one practice drill.

Reveal the task answer and reasoning

A useful drill resembles the missed skill. Knight-attack practice will not directly fix an error caused by entering a drawn pawn ending.

The mistake to watch for

Treating every loss as proof that you need more opening memorization.
