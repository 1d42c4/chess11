About 10–15 minutes with the task · Read, attempt, then explain

The idea

En passant is available immediately after an adjacent enemy pawn advances two squares from its starting square and lands beside your pawn on its fifth rank. The capturing pawn moves diagonally to the passed-over square and the advanced pawn is removed. The resulting position must leave your king safe. Promotion happens on the last rank and is immediately to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight of the same color.

Worked explanation

An en passant capture removes a pawn from a square other than the destination. That can open a rook’s rank and expose a king, making an otherwise plausible capture illegal.

Try it yourself

Set up an en passant position and physically remove both relevant pawns from their old squares before placing the capturing pawn on its destination.

Reveal the task answer and reasoning

Only the just-advanced pawn is captured. Waiting a turn loses the en passant opportunity. Promotion is part of the pawn move, not a later turn.

The mistake to watch for

Leaving the captured en passant pawn on the mental board.
