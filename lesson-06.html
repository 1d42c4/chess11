About 10–15 minutes with the task · Read, attempt, then explain

The idea

A move is illegal if it leaves your own king attacked. This includes moving a pinned piece off a critical line, failing to answer check, and moving the king onto an attacked square. Kings also attack adjacent squares, so they cannot stand next to one another. Test the position after the proposed move, because moving or capturing a blocker can reveal a new attack.

Worked explanation

A White king cannot capture a Black piece protected by a Black pawn. Count the pawn’s attack even if the pawn appears pinned to its own king: for king-move legality, the square is still attacked under the laws of chess.

Try it yourself

Find three king captures in a diagram. For each, remove the captured piece mentally and trace every enemy attack to the king’s destination.

Reveal the task answer and reasoning

A king capture is allowed only if the resulting square is not attacked. The fact that a capture wins material is irrelevant to legality.

The mistake to watch for

Checking the old board instead of the board after the capture.

Close the lesson and test your recall

When do you test king safety? In the resulting position after the entire move has been made.
